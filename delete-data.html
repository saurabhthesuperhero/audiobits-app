Delete your AudioBits data

App: AudioBits (Saikaizen Innovations).

In the app

  1. Signed in: open Profile → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account (name, email, account ID) immediately.
  2. Local data (progress, favourites, streak, settings): open Profile → Reset, or uninstall the app.

By email

Email superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "AudioBits data deletion". We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted and kept

Deleted: your account and the analytics identifier linked to it. Analytics data that is not linked to you is kept for up to 14 months and then removed automatically. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law; manage subscriptions in Google Play.
