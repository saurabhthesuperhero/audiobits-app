The service

AudioBits offers original audio companions that explain and discuss the ideas of books, in Hindi and English. They are not the authors' own text and not official summaries. Narration is generated by a synthetic voice on your device.

Free and Premium

  • Free: the first episode of every book, one full book of your choice each week, and Shorts.
  • Premium is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) sold through Google Play. The price is shown before you buy.
  • It renews automatically until you cancel in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions). Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
