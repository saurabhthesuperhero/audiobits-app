AudioBits Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Developer: Saikaizen Innovations. Contact: superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com

Summary

AudioBits plays long-form audio companions to books in Hindi and English. Narration is generated on your phone: book text and audio never leave the device. You can use the app without an account.

What is stored on your device only

  • Listening progress, favourites, history, streak, daily goal and settings.
  • Downloaded narrator voices and the audio files the app creates while playing.

This data is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app or using Reset in the app deletes it.

What is collected and by whom

ServiceDataWhy
Google Firebase Authentication (optional sign-in with Google)Name, email address, account IDTo keep your purchases and account if you choose to sign in
Google Firebase AnalyticsApp interactions (screens opened, app starts), approximate location from IP (country), device model and OS, app instance IDTo understand which features are used and fix problems
RevenueCat and Google Play BillingPurchase history, subscription status, an app user IDTo process and restore subscriptions

We do not sell personal data and do not show ads. Data is encrypted in transit. Google's and RevenueCat's own policies also apply: Google, RevenueCat.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account deletes your sign-in account. Reset clears all local data.
  • Or follow these steps, or email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Children

AudioBits is meant for teenagers and adults (13+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data use changes.
